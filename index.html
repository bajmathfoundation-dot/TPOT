About T-POT & SNACKS

At T-POT & SNACKS, we specialize in authentic homemade comfort food made with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients right here in Gombe.

Our name "T-POT" represents our signature tea service that has been passed down through generations. Every dish we serve is prepared with love and care, just like grandma used to make.

Whether you're looking for a quick snack, a hearty meal, or a place to celebrate a special occasion, T-POT offers a warm, welcoming atmosphere where everyone feels like family.

Visit us opposite Gombe State University, Second Gate, and experience the taste of authentic Nigerian cuisine with our unique T-POT tea service.

Our Specialties

Kibabs

Our famous meat kibab served with hot tea in an actual T-pot. Made with tender beef, fresh vegetables, and a secret blend of herbs and spices.

Pounded Yam

Fresh and delicious made on order, served with your choice of rich options of stew, agoshi soup, vegetables soup.

Pepper Soups

Spicy and flavorful pepper soups from cow legs, kayan ciki, and other special ingredients.

Loaded Chips

Crispy potato chips loaded with cheese, beef, vegetables, and our special sauce for the ultimate snack experience.

Special Rice

Our signature rice dish cooked with a blend of spices and served with your choice of protein and vegetables.

Masa

Traditional rice cakes served with delicious cow leg soup for an authentic Northern Nigerian experience.
